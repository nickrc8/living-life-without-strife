About the show

Real conversations with friends, teammates, family and classmates about health, sports, food, school and how to be happy without burning yourself out. The world is not perfect, but we have the 101 on how to thrive. New episodes monthly.

Topics: nutrition and processed food, student athletes, football, lacrosse, field hockey, running, injury recovery, culture and food, mindset, and campus life at Pitzer College.
